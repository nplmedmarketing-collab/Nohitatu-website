Chapter 01 · Overview

Medical billing services built to improve revenue performance.

Nohitatu’s goal is to help healthcare providers receive payment for the services they deliver. Our team understands the full revenue cycle — and how accurate registration, coding, claims, and follow-up improve practice performance.

Get paid faster. Error-free data capture, clean claim submission, and timely A/R follow-up reduce delays — so reimbursements move with fewer avoidable denials.

What RCM covers

End-to-end medical billing support across front-end verification, mid-cycle coding and claims, and back-end recovery.

  • Patient registration & demographic entry
  • Medical coding, charge entry & claims submission
  • Payment posting, A/R follow-up & denial management
  • Eligibility, credentialing, indexing & data conversion
Chapter 02 · Services

Complete medical billing & RCM capabilities

From the first patient record through payment posting and denial recovery, each service is designed to protect revenue and shorten days in A/R.

  • 01

    Patient registration / demographic entry

    Our team focuses on accurate capture of patient details. Demographic data forms the base of the medical record and directly affects insurance claim payment — error-free capture supports clean claim submission and faster payer processing.

    Accurate demographics Clean claims Faster processing
  • 02

    Medical coding services

    Accurate medical coding reduces denials and helps generate more revenue. Our experienced, certified coders maintain a high level of accuracy while meeting industry standards — without compromising quality.

    Certified coders Fewer denials Higher accuracy
  • 03

    Charge entry services

    Reimbursement depends on charges entered for services performed. We review medical services carefully, collaborate with coding teams, and capture charges accurately to reduce denials and limit revenue leakage.

    Accurate charges Coding collaboration Less leakage
  • 04

    Electronic claims submission

    We review claims in the practice management system and clearinghouse interface before submission. Work edits and rejects are corrected prior to filing so payers receive cleaner claims.

    Clearinghouse review Edit correction Cleaner filing
  • 05

    Payment posting

    Payment posting shows how effective your revenue cycle is — and, at scale, supports reimbursement trends and analytics. We post patient payments, manual EOBs, denials, and correspondence with accuracy and according to your procedures.

    Patient payments EOB posting Denial posting
  • 06

    Accounts receivable follow-up

    We help practices reduce days in A/R with timely follow-up — contacting payers via website, fax, and phone, monitoring aging buckets, and acting on claim status with refiles, appeals, and focused analytics.

    Aging monitoring Payer follow-up Appeals & refiles
  • 07

    Denial management

    Seasoned specialists investigate every denied claim, correct codes, attach clinical documentation, appeal when needed, and use analytics to reduce recurring denial causes across the revenue cycle.

    Root-cause review Appeals Denial analytics
  • 08

    Insurance eligibility verification

    Many denials stem from eligibility gaps. Before the visit, we verify coverage and benefits with primary and secondary payers, clarify patient responsibility, initiate prior authorizations when required, and update your RCM or practice management system.

    Coverage checks Benefits & copays Prior auth support
  • 09

    Physician credentialing services

    Enrollment and attestation confirm a physician is in a payer’s network and authorized to treat plan members. We support credentialing by helping verify license, experience, certification, and education against payer standards.

    Network enrollment Attestation Payer standards
  • 10

    Indexing medical records

    We organize and store patient demographic and treatment information so staff can access what they need quickly — supporting care decisions and compliant healthcare document management.

    Organized records Fast access Compliance support
  • 11

    Data conversion

    When practices migrate from one billing system to another, our conversion specialists help move data accurately and reliably so operations continue with minimal disruption.

    System migration Accurate conversion Reliable handoff
