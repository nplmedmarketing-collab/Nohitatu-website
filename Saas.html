Chapter 01 · What is SaaS

Why SaaS matters for modern products.

Software-as-a-Service is changing how businesses build, deliver, and scale software. Rather than shipping boxed installs, SaaS lets you deliver continuously from the cloud — and Nohitatu helps you do it with modern architecture, UX, and cloud engineering.

Cloud-ready from day one. Whether you are launching a new SaaS product or evolving an existing one, we pair multi-tenant architecture with security, UX, and cloud practices that keep you ready to grow.

What SaaS unlocks

A delivery model built for continuous improvement, shared infrastructure, and elastic scale.

  • Deploy via the cloud with seamless updates
  • Serve multiple tenants on shared infrastructure
  • Scale usage without re-architecting from scratch
  • Focus on core value while we handle platform, security, and maintenance
Chapter 02 · Our process

Transparent, collaborative, agile delivery

We follow Scrum so you get working software in short iterative cycles — with quick feedback, continuous improvement, and room to adapt as the product evolves.

  • 01

    Discovery & requirement analysis

    We dive deep into your idea, business model, target users, competitors, and technology requirements before a single line of production code.

    Business model Users & competitors Tech requirements
  • 02

    Design & prototyping

    UX/UI designs, wireframes, and clickable prototypes to validate flows early and keep stakeholders aligned.

    UX/UI Wireframes Clickable prototypes
  • 03

    Architecture planning

    We define the technical stack, multi-tenant strategy, database modelling, and API / microservices design for long-term scale.

    Multi-tenant APIs Microservices
  • 04

    Development & testing (Scrum sprints)

    Iterative sprints deliver incremental, testable product versions every cycle — with transparent updates, flexibility to adapt, and faster delivery of working features.

    Scrum sprints Continuous feedback Working software
  • 05

    Deployment & scaling

    Launch on cloud platforms with monitoring, scaling strategies, backups, and disaster recovery built into the plan.

    Cloud launch Monitoring DR & backups
  • 06

    Support, maintenance & improvement

    After launch: continuous feedback integration, versioning, and performance optimization so your product stays ahead.

    Feedback loops Versioning Optimization
